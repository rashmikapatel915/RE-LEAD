# RE:LEAD — Sales Memory & Learning Agent

## Problem
Sales teams often lose valuable knowledge from previous customer interactions.
When a similar lead appears later, the team may repeat the same mistakes.

## Solution
RE:LEAD is an AI sales memory agent that remembers previous sales interactions,
recalls similar historical experiences, and uses them to recommend the next action.

## How Hindsight Is Used

Hindsight is the memory layer of RE:LEAD.

1. Sales interactions are retained as memories.
2. When a new/stalled lead is analyzed, RE:LEAD recalls relevant past experiences.
3. The retrieved memories are shown to the sales representative.
4. These historical experiences help guide the recommended next action.

## Core Workflow

New Lead
↓
Customer Information
↓
Hindsight Memory
↓
Recall Similar Sales Experiences
↓
Recommendation
↓
Sales Outcome
↓
New Memory
